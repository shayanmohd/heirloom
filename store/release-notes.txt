Recording works.

In the last version the big button could never open the microphone, on any phone, so pressing it always ended in an error screen. That is fixed. Press the button, talk, press Done, and the story is kept with the ring the voice drew.

If the microphone was refused twice, Android stops asking, and the error screen now has a button that opens this app's settings page where it can be allowed again.

Every story already on this phone is exactly where you left it.
