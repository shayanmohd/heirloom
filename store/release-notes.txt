First release.

One question a week for someone you love, answered out loud and kept in their own voice. Three hundred
hand-written prompts across seven packs, including India and South Asia, Britain and Ireland, and one for
anyone who built a life in a country they were not born in.

Elder mode is one screen with one big button. Recordings run up to thirty minutes.

No account, no cloud, no transcription, no internet permission.
